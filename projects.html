projects

Each name links to its repo on GitHub. Language in brackets.

  • unidex (C) - pocket e-ink OS for the Waveshare ESP32-S3 1.54" board. Timetable, voice notes, a pet, a few games. Latest is v1.14.
  • unidex-apps (Lua) - apps and games for unidex, plus the guides for writing your own. Hop, Chooser and Climate are in here.
  • unidex-learn (JavaScript) - coding courses at learn.unidex.shop. C++ for beginners, make your own app (12 lessons), make your own game (8).
  • FLP (TypeScript) - Forrest Labs Play, a media player for macOS with plugins. 0.1.1 needs macOS 26.
  • cammap (JavaScript) - a hand-kept, read-only map of London. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • skillotchi (JavaScript) - a Tamagotchi-style creature that lives on real instrument practice, picked up from the mic's pitch. Browser prototype, with a live demo.
  • ForrestOS (C++) - phone-style firmware for the Cheap Yellow Display. 20+ apps and a plain-text browser.
  • vision (Python) - camera instance segmentation and face recognition.
  • forrest-notes (C) - firmware for a voice-note device. Transcribes what you record and syncs it as Markdown.
  • clap-to-open (Python) - clap and your whole workspace relaunches, every app on the right monitor.
  • Flipper-Zero-All (HTML) - a pile of files for the Flipper Zero. Own hardware only.
