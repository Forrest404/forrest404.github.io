Battery stuff in 1.14

29 Oct

v1.14 is out, with a battery saver. Most of today went on something else though: the battery percentage while charging.

The saver is a switch in Settings. With it on, the device sleeps after 5 seconds at most, and the Pet stops blinking and doing reaction frames. The Clock only counts every 10 seconds (every second for the last 10 of a timer). The phone portal turns itself off after 3 minutes idle, or 20 at most. The radio runs at 160 MHz except during the TLS handshake.

First bug: on USB the percentage sat at about 84 and never got to 100. The firmware does a steady 40-minute climb to 100 once the voltage reads 4.19 V. But ADCs differ between boards, and mine reads the charger's 4.20 V as 4.158 V, so the climb never started. Now it also starts once the reading has stopped rising (by 8 mV) for 10 minutes above 4.10 V. battery_test covers both cases: a board reading 40 mV low has to go through a whole charge to 100% and never early, and a reading stuck at 4.158 V on USB has to reach 100%.

Second one: after a restart, or an update while plugged in, the percentage started around 81 and climbed for 40 minutes. The estimate gets lost when the firmware changes, so a full battery on USB was being treated as 81%. Now a board that starts up on USB and looks full counts as at least 95%. Topping up takes time in proportion to what's left: 40 minutes from 85, about 13 from 95. On the board it showed 95% straight away.

CI had been red on beta and main since the battery saver went in. The browser emulator didn't have the new powerSaver() and powerSetSaver() functions, so it stopped building. I added them. The Try page also retries a release's emulator with the current checkout's stand-ins when it can't build its own, since v1.14 was tagged before those functions existed.
